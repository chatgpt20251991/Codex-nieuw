# Go-To-Market — EUBatteryPassport.nl

## Positioning
Do not sell "a QR code".
Do not sell "71 fields".
Sell removal of operational compliance burden.

Core proposition:
**From supplier evidence to a managed EU Battery Passport.**

## Initial ICP
Start where pain is high but procurement is not OEM-slow:
1. EU importers of LMT batteries / e-bike, e-scooter battery packs
2. BESS / home & commercial storage importers and brands
3. Industrial battery pack manufacturers/importers
4. Battery assemblers placing finished batteries on the EU market
5. EV-adjacent specialty/low-volume manufacturers after the workflow is proven

## Wedge offer
### Battery Passport Readiness Sprint
- scope determination
- 71-point mapping
- supplier evidence gap
- technical systems map
- risk register
- implementation plan
Deliverable: dashboard, not a static PDF.

Then convert to:
### Managed Battery Passport Platform
- supplier chasing
- evidence management
- passport generation
- hosting
- lifecycle updates
- registry submission when operational
- audit support

## Pricing direction
Avoid pure low-cost commodity SaaS competition.
Public competitors in 2026 already advertise:
- self-serve plans around €149/month
- higher-volume plans around €449/month
- enterprise from ~€1,290/month
- specialist DBP annual platforms from about €11,900/year upward

Our differentiation should justify managed pricing:
- onboarding/setup
- supplier workflow
- evidence provenance
- managed compliance
- registry authorisation + submission
- legal rule updates
- enterprise lifecycle/telemetry

Proposed early offer for design partners:
- Readiness Sprint: €1,500–€3,500
- Implementation: €3,500–€12,500 depending on integrations/data quality
- Managed Platform: €750–€3,000+/month depending on passport volume and service level
- Custom enterprise/API/SLA: quote

Do not lock final pricing until 5–10 customer interviews validate willingness to pay.

## Moat
Commodity generators will race to cents per passport.
Moat must be:
- evidence graph
- supplier collection network
- rules/version engine
- managed authorisation/registry workflow
- lifecycle data
- auditability
- integration speed
- continuity guarantees
- regulatory update speed

## Sales message
"Your QR code is the easy part. The hard part is proving every value behind it, keeping the passport correct for years, controlling restricted data, and getting each battery through the EU Registry. We run that entire layer."

## First sales asset
Free/low-friction "Battery Passport Risk Scan":
- 10 questions
- determine scope
- estimate % data already available
- identify top 5 blockers
- CTA to readiness sprint
