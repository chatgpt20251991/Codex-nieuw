Use the online intake or info@eubatterypassport.nl for a short initial enquiry. Do not send confidential technical documents, identity documents or passwords at this stage. Agree on a suitable transfer route first.

A model dossier can supply information for multiple batteries. An individual battery is distinct from its model. Preparation, publication and registration remain separate steps.

Assess passport obligations and industrial performance requirements separately. The passport deadline remains 18 February 2027. New performance minimums in the Commission proposal of 29 September 2026 are not yet applicable requirements. Article 10(2) ties their application to the later of 18 August 2027 or 18 months after the final delegated act enters into force. The exclusively external storage exception under performance minimums is not a general passport exemption. Existing documentation requirements need a separate assessment.
